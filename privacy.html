The short version

Pinglight collects nothing and transmits nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party SDK. The app ships with no network code at all — not a setting, an architectural fact — so nothing the app hears or shows can leave your phone.

1. What we collect

Nothing. We operate no server, database, or backend for Pinglight, and the app has no way to send us (or anyone) anything. We never receive a device identifier, an IP address, a usage event, a crash log, or any signal from your device. We do not track, so we do not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework — there is nothing to ask permission for.

2. Bluetooth scanning stays on your phone

Pinglight asks for Bluetooth permission so it can listen to the public advertisement broadcasts that nearby devices already transmit. Everything it hears is processed on your phone against registries built into the app. Scan results are shown to you and to no one else. The session log is held in memory only and is erased when you close the app. Pinglight only listens to broadcasts; it never connects to any device.

The only data written to local storage is your app settings, inside the app's own sandboxed container. Deleting the app deletes everything. Nothing was ever copied anywhere else, so there is nothing for us to delete on request — we never had anything.

3. Purchases are handled by Apple

Pinglight is a one-time paid app sold through the App Store. Your Apple ID, payment method, and purchase record are handled entirely by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy and are not shared with us. Refunds are handled by Apple.

4. Children's privacy (COPPA)

Because we collect no personal information from any user of any age, Pinglight does not collect personal information from children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect, use, or disclose children's personal information as defined by COPPA. There are no accounts, no chat, and no way to submit information to us through the app.

5. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA / CPRA and similar laws)

Data protection laws give you rights over your personal data. We hold no personal data about you to act on. Nothing about you or your scans ever reaches us, so there is nothing to access, correct, delete, export, or stop selling — we have never sold or shared personal information, and structurally cannot. If you send a data request to the address below, our accurate and complete response is written confirmation that we hold nothing. No cross-border transfer of personal data occurs, no lawful basis under Article 6 GDPR is engaged, and there is no data on any server of ours to breach.

6. Changes to this policy

If a future version of Pinglight ever adds a feature that involves data leaving the device, we will update this document, its effective date, and the App Store privacy label before that version ships.
